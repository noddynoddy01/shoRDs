## Finding a mentor
Open the Mentors tab. It lists verified mentors only. Search by keyword, filter by research domain, expertise, skill, institution or availability, and sort the results. Open a mentor to read their public profile and see how many open places they have.

## Asking for mentorship
On a mentor's profile choose Request mentorship. Say which research domain you need help with, describe your interests and what guidance you want. You need a completed profile. You can have only one pending or active request with the same mentor. A pending request can be cancelled. Statuses are: Pending, Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled and Completed. The mentor may decline without giving a reason; a request is not a guarantee.

## When a mentor accepts
Accepting creates the mentorship and a private conversation between the two of you, and both get a notification. Either person can mark the mentorship as completed later; the conversation then stays readable but you can no longer add messages.

## Becoming a mentor
Finish your profile, then open the mentor application from the Mentors tab. Describe your background, expertise, the areas you can mentor in, relevant experience, portfolio links, availability and how many mentees you can take. An administrator reviews it. You appear in the directory only after approval. You can be rejected and apply again, and an administrator can later remove verification. Mentors manage requests and active mentees from the mentorship screens and set their availability on their profile.
