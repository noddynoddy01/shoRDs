## Messaging
Messages are text only (up to 2,000 characters). You can message your mentor or mentee in the private conversation created when a mentorship is accepted. You can also start a direct conversation with someone who has turned on "accept direct messages" in their profile. You can read your conversations from the messages icon. The app checks for new messages every few seconds while a conversation is open; it is not instant push messaging, so a reply may take a few seconds to appear.

## Read status
Unread conversations show a count. When the other person opens a conversation, your messages in it show as read.

## Blocking and reporting
You can block a person from their conversation: neither of you can send new messages afterwards, and old messages stay. You can also report a conversation or a message; the report is stored for an administrator to review. Nobody else can read your conversations.

## Notifications
Notifications are in-app only: a list opened from the bell icon with an unread count. You get them for new mentorship requests, accepted or declined requests, started or completed mentorships and new messages. Mark one or all as read from that list. shoRDs does not send email or phone push notifications.
