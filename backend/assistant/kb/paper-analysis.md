## How to analyse a paper
Sign in, open a paper from the feed or search, find the "Cited AI analysis" panel and press "Run AI analysis". Nothing runs until you press the button. It uses part of your daily AI allowance and takes about a minute; while it runs you can press "Stop waiting" (the job may still finish on the server). The panel shows progress: getting ready, waiting for a free slot, analysing.

## What you get
A list of statements about the paper's problem, method, findings and limitations. Each statement points to the sentence of the paper it came from; open the evidence view to read that sentence. Statements that failed the automatic checks are removed and the panel says how many and why.

## What the labels mean
Results are marked Unverified: the cited excerpt was checked mechanically (it exists in the paper), but nobody and nothing has verified that it logically supports the statement. Always read the original for anything important. If the paper's licence does not allow showing its text, the statements are shown but the source sentence is withheld and a link to the source is given.

## When analysis is not possible
- The record has no DOI, PMID or arXiv id: it cannot be analysed.
- Only metadata or no content is available: the app says so and no AI is used.
- Signed out: sign in first.
- Too many requests or the daily allowance is used: wait and try again later.
- The AI service may be temporarily paused by the shoRDs administrator.
