## Main tabs
The bar at the bottom has six tabs: Home (the paper feed), Explore, Upload, Mentors, Contact and Profile. On wide screens a side menu also lists Feed, Explore, Search and Profile.

## Where things are
- Paper feed: Home tab.
- Searching for papers: Explore tab, or the Search screen.
- Mentor directory: Mentors tab. Mentorship requests and your active mentorships are linked from the Mentors tab.
- Messages: the messages icon at the top of the main screens opens your conversations.
- Notifications: the bell icon at the top of the main screens shows the list and the unread count.
- Your profile and settings: Profile tab (edit profile from there).
- Projects: the Projects screen is opened from the Research Copilot page.
- The shoRDs Assistant: the round help button on the main screens, or the Assistant screen.
- Contact the shoRDs owner: Contact tab (opens your email app).
The Research Copilot is separate from the Assistant and opens from a paper.
