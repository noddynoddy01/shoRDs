## What the Assistant can do
It explains what shoRDs is, how to use each feature, how to search and what DOI, arXiv and PMID references are, how analysis and evidence labels work, how projects, profiles and mentorship work, and how to fix common problems. It can also explain common research terms and suggest how to start with research as a beginner.

## What it cannot do
It cannot read your papers, answer scientific questions about a specific paper (use the Research Copilot or the analysis), see your messages or other users' profiles, change settings for you, or take actions in the app. It only knows what its product guide says; when the guide does not cover something it says it does not know.

## Your conversations
Conversations are saved to your account so you can reopen them. You can start a new conversation, delete one, or clear your whole history. Only you can see them. Each answer is produced by an AI model and counts toward the shoRDs AI budget, so it can be paused by the administrator.
