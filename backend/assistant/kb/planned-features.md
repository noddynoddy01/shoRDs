## Not available today
Do not tell anyone these work. They are planned or incomplete:
- Analysing an uploaded PDF (the Upload tab does not read PDFs).
- Payments, subscriptions or a paywall (everything is free; there is no billing).
- The research canvas and review mode (these screens only say "not available yet").
- Email or phone push notifications (only the in-app list exists).
- Sending files or images in messages (text only).
- Instant push messaging (the app polls for new messages every few seconds).
- A password-reset flow and email verification screens.
- Sign-in with Google in the native mobile app (web only).
- Comparing or reasoning over several papers at once in the Research Copilot.
- Paper sharing or publishing to other users from the Upload tab.
- Choosing AI providers other than the one the app uses.
If asked whether one of these exists, say clearly that it is not available yet.
