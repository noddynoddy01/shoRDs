## Is shoRDs free?
Yes. There are no payments or subscriptions in the app right now. AI features have a daily allowance per user.

## Who can see my messages and conversations?
Only the people in the conversation. Administrators can see a message only if someone reports it. The Assistant cannot see your messages.

## Who can see my profile?
Signed-in people can see your public profile after you finish onboarding. Your email is never shown. You choose whether to accept direct messages.

## Does the AI always give correct results?
No. Results are labelled Unverified and are checked only mechanically against the paper's text. Read the original for anything important.

## Can I use the app offline?
Saved papers can be kept on your device, but sign-in, search, analysis, messaging and the Assistant need a connection.

## How do I become a verified mentor?
Finish your profile and submit the mentor application; an administrator approves or rejects it.
