## What the Research Copilot is
The Research Copilot answers questions about one specific paper, using that paper's own text, and cites the excerpts it used. It is a separate feature from the shoRDs Assistant. The Assistant explains the app; the Copilot works on paper content.

## How to open it
In the web version it opens from the paper cards on wider screens. It is a web-only screen at the moment; the mobile app does not have an entry point to it. Select a paper, type a question and send.

## Limits
It handles one paper at a time (no multi-paper reasoning), it does not remember previous conversations, and answers are labelled unverified. Some buttons for inspecting evidence are not finished yet. Questions that need information outside the paper are outside what it can answer.
