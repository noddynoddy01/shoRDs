## Browsing and searching
The Home tab shows a live feed of recent papers. The Explore tab and the Search screen let you search by topic or keyword. Search results come from the OpenAlex scholarly index; shoRDs does not invent papers and does not fill the feed with placeholders.

## References shoRDs understands
To analyse or open a specific paper, shoRDs needs a DOI, a PubMed id (PMID) or an arXiv id. A DOI looks like 10.1038/nature14539 and can be pasted with or without https://doi.org/ in front. An arXiv id looks like 2301.01234. A PMID is a number from PubMed. A paper record with none of these cannot be analysed.

## The Upload tab
The Upload tab can fetch a paper's details from a link or DOI and keep a brief on your device. It does NOT read or analyse PDF files, and it does not publish anything to other users. Analysis of an uploaded PDF is a planned feature and is not available.

## Open access and full text
Some papers only have an abstract or metadata available. When full text cannot be used, an analysis may be limited to the abstract or may be refused with a message that the content is unavailable.
