## Editing your profile
Open the Profile tab and choose Edit profile. You can change your name, photo link, profile type, headline, short bio, institution, department, academic level, research interests and domains, skills, expertise, ORCID, LinkedIn, GitHub, website and publication links, your availability, and whether you accept direct messages. Almost every field is optional.

## What other people can see
Other signed-in users can see your public profile fields once you have finished onboarding. Your email address is never shown to other people. Direct messages from people you have no mentorship with are off by default; you can switch them on in your profile.

## What you cannot change yourself
You cannot make yourself a verified mentor or an administrator by editing your profile. Verified mentors are approved by an administrator after an application.

## App appearance
The Settings tray on the Profile tab lets you choose a theme and text size. Subscriptions and payments are not available; all features are free for now.
