## Creating an account and signing in
Open the app and choose Enter shoRDs. On the sign-in screen you can create an account with a name, an email address and a password (at least 6 characters), or sign in with an existing account. Google sign-in works in the web version only. There is no sign-in with Apple, phone number or GitHub.

## Things that are not available at sign-in
There is currently no "forgot password" flow in the app and no email-verification step. If someone cannot sign in, check the email and password, check the connection, and see the troubleshooting guide. Do not promise a password reset.

## First-time profile setup (onboarding)
After the first sign-in the app asks you to set up your shoRDs profile: choose what describes you (student, mentor, researcher, professor/academic, or independent research enthusiast), add your name and a few research interests, and optionally more (institution, skills, links). You can save progress and finish later, and you can edit your profile at any time from the Profile tab. Choosing "mentor" does not make you a verified mentor; that needs an application and administrator approval.

## Is my account the same as my profile?
Your sign-in account is handled by Firebase. Your shoRDs profile (name, interests, persona, links) is stored separately by shoRDs. Your email address is never shown to other people.
